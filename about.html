About

What this is

Wake Up is a single argument, printed large: results follow from turning up, not from waiting until you feel like it. The copy is deliberately blunt, which is why it is set in a formal serif instead of a poster face — the tension between the two is the whole design.

The text on the front page was not written for this site. It comes from a post published on Instagram, kept here word for word apart from the spelling.

Colophon

Display and text
Cormorant Garamond, at 300, 400, 400 italic and 700.
Handwritten cuts
Caveat, flattened from its variable source to the single weight the site uses.
Font delivery
Self-hosted, subset to Latin and served as WOFF2. Five files, 175 KB in total.
Build step
None. Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served as written.
Dependencies
None. No framework, no package manager and nothing loaded from a CDN.
Tracking
None. The site sets no cookies and runs no third-party script.
Motion
CSS animation only, and it stops entirely under prefers-reduced-motion.

Who made it

Written, designed and built by Pablo Nieto Pérez. The source is public, and the site is a portfolio piece as much as it is a manifesto.
