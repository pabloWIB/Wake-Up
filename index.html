The manifesto

You might not get a second chance. Don’t f*ck up.

Going through the motions

Wake up, and get sh*t done.

It doesn’t matter if you just did one thing. It doesn’t matter if you did ten.

What matters is that you went through the motions regardless of how you felt when you woke up — and that simple act of going through the motions is what gets you one step closer to whatever you are trying to achieve.

One step. Today. That is the whole method.
